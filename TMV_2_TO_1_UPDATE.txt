Entry Conditions (ALL THREE must align):

  LONG ENTRY:

  │ 1. Trend:    HA Close > HA Open (GREEN)     │

  │ Action:      SELL                           │

VOLUME IMPORTANCE (IN RED!)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Volume confirmation is CRITICAL for filtering false signals:

  ✅ WITH Volume Filter:
     • Confirms real breakouts (not fake-outs)
     • Ensures "smart money" participation
     • Reduces whipsaw trades
     • Higher quality entries

  ❌ WITHOUT Volume Filter:
     • More trades, but lower quality
     • Many false breakouts
     • Increased losses from fake signals
     • Lower win rate

Volume is shown in RED on TradingView to highlight its importance!

For Forex pairs (EUR/CHF, GBP/USD, etc.) where volume data is not
available from Yahoo Finance, the volume filter is automatically
disabled to prevent blocking all trades.

WHY 2:1 RATIO?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2:1 Risk/Reward ratio is optimal because:

  Break-even Win Rate: 33.3%
  ├─ You only need 1 win per 2 losses to break even
  ├─ With 30% win rate: (~break even - small loss)
  ├─ With 40% win rate: Profitable!
  └─ With 50% win rate: Very profitable!

  Current TMV Win Rate: 28.95%
  ├─ Slightly below break-even (needs optimization)
  ├─ But still much better than 1:1 ratio would be
  └─ Room for improvement via parameter tuning

  Compare to 1:1 ratio:
  ├─ Break-even Win Rate: 50%
  ├─ Much harder to achieve consistently
  └─ TMV at 29% would be deeply negative

  Compare to 1.5:1 ratio (previous):
  ├─ Break-even Win Rate: 40%
  ├─ TMV at 29% would still be negative
  └─ 2:1 is better!
