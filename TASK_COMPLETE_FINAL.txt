📊 Trend + Momentum + Volume (TMV) ⭐ DEFAULT
   ├─ Rules: Smoothed Heikin Ashi (50/10) + Range Filter (100, 3.0) + VolSMA(30)
   ├─ Entry: HA BULLISH + Range Filter BUY/LONG + Volume > Average
   └─ Exit: HA BEARISH OR Range Filter SELL OR SL/TP

📊 Mean Reversion Strategy
   ├─ Rules: Bollinger Bands (20, 2.0) + RSI (14, 30)
   ├─ Entry: Price ≤ Lower Band AND RSI < 30
   └─ Exit: Price ≥ Middle Band OR RSI > 50

📊 Trend Following Strategy
   ├─ Rules: SMA Crossover (50/200) + MACD confirmation
   ├─ Entry: Close > SMA200 AND SMA50 > SMA200 AND MACD hist > 0
   └─ Exit: Close < SMA50 OR MACD histogram < 0

📊 Double RSI
   ├─ Rules: Trend RSI(21) + Signal RSI(7)
   ├─ Entry: Trend RSI > 60 AND Signal RSI crosses above 40
   └─ Exit: Trend RSI < 40 OR Signal RSI crosses below 60
